# Job Responsibility Evaluation Guidance

Evaluate responsibilities by comparing the vacancy's explicit duties with the candidate's documented, job-related activities.

- Prefer evidence that describes comparable tasks, scope, tools, or outcomes.
- Distinguish a responsibility from a related skill: knowing a tool does not by itself prove that the candidate performed the duty.
- Treat general role titles or broad statements as context, not proof of every responsibility.
- Mark responsibilities as supported, partial, or not evidenced when the available profile information is limited.

Use the vacancy wording as the authority for required responsibilities. General competency frameworks can provide context but must not create unlisted requirements.
