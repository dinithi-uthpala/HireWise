# Experience Evaluation Guidelines

Evaluate experience against the vacancy's explicit role, duration, level, and domain requirements.

- Give strongest support to documented experience performing comparable work.
- Consider projects, internships, placements, or volunteer work only when the vacancy accepts that type of experience or the requirement is otherwise broad.
- Use dates, duration, role scope, and described responsibilities when available; a job title alone is insufficient evidence.
- Separate directly relevant experience from transferable or adjacent experience and explain the distinction.
- Do not infer seniority, duration, or domain exposure from an unstated assumption.

Missing experience evidence is a gap or warning. It must not be silently converted into either a match or a rejection.
