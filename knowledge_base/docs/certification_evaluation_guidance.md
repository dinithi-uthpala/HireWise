# Certification Evaluation Guidance

Evaluate certifications only when they are stated in the vacancy or are clearly relevant supporting evidence for a stated requirement.

- Compare the documented certification name with the vacancy's explicit certification requirement.
- Record a certification as supported only when the candidate profile contains the certification or a clearly documented equivalent.
- Do not infer a certification from a related skill, course, degree, job title, or work activity.
- Preserve uncertainty when the profile does not provide the certification name, status, or other required detail.
- Treat preferred certifications as supporting evidence and do not turn them into mandatory requirements.

Certification evidence supplements the vacancy criteria; it does not change the configured Agent 2 scoring formula.
