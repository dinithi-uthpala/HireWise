# Job Requirement Evidence Rules

Agent 2 should connect each evaluated requirement to explicit vacancy language and documented candidate-profile evidence.

- Use explicit requirements as the primary source for what must be evaluated.
- Classify evidence as supported, partially supported, missing, or conflicting when the profile permits that distinction.
- Keep candidate evidence separate from general role guidance and retrieval context.
- Do not treat an absent profile field as proof that a candidate lacks the requirement.
- Report missing or conflicting evidence as a gap or warning rather than inventing details.

Evidence summaries should remain job-related, traceable to the available fields, and concise enough for human review.
