# HR Assistant Competency Framework

## Role scope

An HR assistant supports structured people-process administration while protecting confidentiality.

## Typical capabilities

- Administrative accuracy and document management
- Communication and teamwork
- Spreadsheet and office-tool use
- Scheduling, record handling, and process follow-through

## Typical responsibilities

- Maintain accurate recruitment and employee-process records.
- Coordinate schedules and communications.
- Apply approved procedures consistently.
- Escalate sensitive or uncertain cases to authorized HR staff.

## Responsible evaluation note

Candidate matching must use job-related evidence only and must not use protected characteristics or personal contact information.
